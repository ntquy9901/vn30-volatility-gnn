## What is GELU?

**GELU (Gaussian Error Linear Unit):**
- Formula: `GELU(x) = x * Φ(x)` where Φ is Gaussian CDF
- Smoother than ReLU: no sharp kink at x=0
- Probabilistic: weighted by Gaussian distribution
- Better gradients: non-zero for negative inputs

**Why GELU for volatility?**
1. Volatility is continuous (not binary like ReLU assumes)
2. Financial time series have smooth regime transitions
3. Negative deviations carry information (ReLU discards these)
4. State-of-the-art for transformers and time series (2026)
